pointless things

sometimes my mind
goes on about
pointless things.

why do i tag the post
both #poem and
#poetry?
as if it could be
one but not
the other.

it seems…
inefficient,
after all, why
do twice
what could be done
once?

but then i
remind myself of
the brutalities done
in the name of
efficiency.

and of all
the things i
#love to do
twice, thrice,
and more.
